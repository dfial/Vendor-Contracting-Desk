          Request→
          Triage→
          Review and negotiate→
          Approve and sign→
          Repository→
          Renewal horizon
          ↺ renewals, amendments and exits come back through triage
        

      

      

        

          
One front door

          
Every purchase starts with the same short intake form in the CLM. The triage rules on the New request tab route it: low-risk buys go through on a checklist the same day, and everything else reaches the right reviewers on day one instead of day ten.

        

        

          
One playbook, in Claude

          
Preferred, fallback and escalation positions live in a Claude skill the whole team uses for first-pass reviews and redlines. The AGC approves changes, and escalations and repeated vendor pushback feed the next version.

        

        

          
A renewal horizon

          
A weekly digest from the CLM repository goes to FinOps and budget owners with every notice window in the next 90 days. Each renewal comes with a brief and a negotiation target, and spend concentration and overlapping tools are flagged.

        

        

          
Guardrails for AI

          
Contract data stays in approved workspaces. Claude drafts, compares and checks; a person makes the call. Every clause Claude quotes is checked against the source document before anything goes to a vendor.

        

      

      

        
My first 90 days

        

          
Days 1–30
Map how buying works today. List the top vendors by spend and every notice date in the repository, and stop anything about to auto-renew by accident.

          
Days 31–60
Ship playbook v1 in Claude and the intake triage with FinOps. Open self-serve paths for NDAs and low-risk tools.

          
Days 61–90
Start the weekly renewal digest and train budget owners. Report the first numbers: cycle time, share of self-serve requests and savings at renewal.
